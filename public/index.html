Every up or down call is checked one trading day later on that sector's fund. Direction is right when the fund beat (or trailed) the market, SPY, the way Ripple said. Size is right when the move also landed in the predicted tier: Small under 0.5%, Medium 0.5 to 1.5%, Big over 1.5% versus the market. A coin flip gets direction right about half the time.
